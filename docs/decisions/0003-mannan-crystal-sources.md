## Mannan I source

The package definition is derived from Chanzy, Perez, Miller, Paradossi, and
Winter, *Macromolecules* **1987**, 20, 2407-2413,
doi:10.1021/ma00176a014. The paper reports an orthorhombic
P2_12_12_1 cell with `a = 8.92`, `b = 7.21`, and `c = 10.27 A`, Cartesian
coordinates for one beta-D-mannose residue, the two-fold chain screw, and the
operation producing the antiparallel chain.

`tools/convert_mannan_i.py` contains the integer coordinate table verbatim and
generates `data/crystallography/mannan/i.json`. The paper's O1 coincides with
the screw-related O4 and therefore represents one glycosidic oxygen twice.
CelluloseForge retains one copy under its O4 convention. Table I omits the
three hydroxyl hydrogens. The converter places them at 0.96 A on the 109.5
degree C-O-H cone, choosing the direction closest to the acceptor in the
reported interchain O2-H...O5 and intrachain O3-H...O5 and O6-H...O3
hydrogen-bond network. Package
provenance explicitly distinguishes these modelled sites from the published
coordinates.

Choosing the individually most-linear hydrogen bonds places neighbouring HO3
and HO6 atoms only 1.37 A apart. The converter instead optimizes the three
hydroxyl torsions together on their fixed 109.5 degree C-O-H cones. This keeps
periodic H...H contacts at or above 1.8 A while retaining the reported
acceptor directions; because the rounded 2.385 A O3...O5 contact prevents an
ideal HO3 geometry, its resulting H...O separation is approximately 1.58 A
and its O-H...O angle approximately 138 degrees. These are explicitly
modelled hydrogen sites. Any later force-field mapping must first perform a
restrained hydrogen relaxation while preserving and then reassessing the
experimental heavy-atom lattice.

The donor-acceptor distances computed directly from the rounded Table I
coordinates are 3.110 A for interchain O2...O5, 2.385 A for intrachain
O3...O5, and 2.711 A for intrachain O6...O3. The first two differ from the
2.93 and 2.58 A values summarized for the X-ray model in later literature;
the package preserves the primary electron-diffraction coordinate table and
records the discrepancy rather than silently moving its heavy atoms.

An alternative coordinate table in Stewart's 2003 thesis was rejected. That
work states that its direct three-dimensional solution was unsuccessful, and
the tabulated model contains chemically impossible covalent distances. It is
not a suitable source for a simulation builder.

## Validation boundary

The Mannan I milestone supports coordinate-only unit cells and periodic bulk
replication. Tests require:

- the published unit cell, P2_12_12_1 symmetry, two antiparallel chains, four
  residues, and 84 atoms per cell;
- beta-D-mannose configuration at C1-C5, including the C2 epimeric difference
  from beta-D-glucose;
- ordinary intramolecular bond lengths and 1.39 A periodic O4-C1 linkages;
- no interchain heavy-atom contact shorter than 3.0 A;
- no periodic hydroxyl-H...H contact shorter than 1.8 A and no nonbonded
  hydroxyl-H...heavy-atom contact shorter than 1.55 A;
- source DOI and conversion provenance in the build report; and
- mirrored structures to fail the stereochemistry check.

Slabs, CHARMM36, OpenMM, LAMMPS, and native Amber output remain disabled for
mannan until their own mapping and engine validations pass.

## Mannan II gate

Mannan II is a hydrated, four-chain orthorhombic polymorph. Cell dimensions
and chain conformation alone are insufficient: a release definition requires
the audited atomic chain packing, primary-hydroxyl orientation, and water
sites from the structural model associated with Millane and Hendrixson,
*Carbohydrate Polymers* **1994**, 25, 245-251,
doi:10.1016/0144-8617(94)90050-7 (or an equivalent primary coordinate
deposit). It must not be generated by merely changing the Mannan I cell.
