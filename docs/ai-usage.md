# AI assistance log

This log supports transparent disclosure of AI assistance during software development and documentation.

## Initial project foundation

- Tool: OpenAI Codex.
- Scope: repository planning, package scaffolding, initial documentation, and test scaffolding.
- Human responsibility: project authors must review, validate, and approve all scientific decisions, implementations, tests, and manuscript text before release.

## SoftwareX manuscript

- Tools: OpenAI Codex, Anthropic Claude, and Consensus.
- Scope: manuscript structure and prose drafting; literature discovery and bibliographic metadata cross-checking for crystallographic source papers, related builders, GLYCAM, AmberTools, and GROMACS.
- Human responsibility: the author must verify the bibliography against publisher records, review every scientific and comparative claim, complete author and funding metadata, and approve the final submission.

## Code review and manuscript editing

- Tool: Anthropic Claude.
- Scope: review of the crystallographic geometry, GLYCAM mapping, and GROMACS output modules against the legacy Cellulose-Builder source and published unit-cell data; comparison of CelluloseForge against Cellulose-Builder and Nanopolysaccharide Builder; targeted edits to manuscript author metadata and the AI-use disclosure.
- Human responsibility: the author reviewed all findings and edits and remains responsible for verifying them before submission.
